Three Member Groups, Three Ways In.

Apprenticeship & Applied Learning

Build fundamentals, judgment and evidence of capability as AI takes over the routine work junior marketers used to learn from.

Participant outcome

A portfolio-ready project, documented feedback, stronger connections, a final presentation, and a possible completion certificate.

Possible pathway
  1. Join a cohort, possibly organized through a SIG.
  2. Set learning goals with an AMA Chicago or organizational mentor.
  3. Complete selected programming, for example one workshop, two Connex events, a Signature Speaker Series event and a Career Connections session.
  4. Apply it to a project (a committee need or a sponsor challenge) with scheduled mentor feedback.
  5. Present the work, decisions, revisions and lessons learned.

How the committees contribute 06

Shared Framing Keeps the Extra Committee Work Small.

Connect the Learning

Use CH to frame existing offerings and link them into the tracks. The first job is choosing suitable offerings, adjusting their framing, and adding a learning or discussion component.

  • Signature Speaker Series: how leaders respond when AI raises output but strains judgment, trust and talent development.
  • Workshops: skills people can apply afterward, like evaluating AI-assisted work.
  • Marketing Immersive: sessions that satisfy cohort milestones.
  • Monthly AI Marketing Forum & Connex: the listening mechanism. Each session tests a CH hypothesis and captures new signals.
  • Mentorship: feedback on applied work across sessions.

Connect the Member Journey

Make the pathways a clear reason to join, take part and renew. Cohort participation becomes a member benefit, with any program fee clearly defined and a bundle option for new members.

Over time members move from emerging marketer to practitioner, then mentor or leader.

  • Explain which opportunity fits each member.
  • Include pathways in orientation and renewal communications.
  • Help participants build relationships within their cohort.
  • Track engagement, completion, renewal and reasons for joining.
  • Explore sponsored places to widen access.

Contribute Challenges, Expertise & Access

Partners play a substantive role in building marketing capability, not just logo placement. A sponsor could set a project challenge for a cohort, borrowing the case-brief approach of AMA National's Collegiate Case Competition.

  • Support research or underwrite cohort programs.
  • Provide expertise, host workshops, give expert feedback.
  • Fund emerging-marketer access.
  • With permission, share the story of what members learned and what the sponsor contributed.
Guardrail

AMA Chicago keeps editorial independence. Sponsorship never determines the findings or the learning standards.

Keep it Consistent

Every opportunity is presented the same way across channels: who it serves, what participants do, what they leave with, and how to join.

  • Communications: one message framework for the website, emails, promotion and events.
  • Insights: a short template for capturing participant experiences and outcomes.
  • DEI: shape access, cultural evaluation and learning content.
  • Joe & Jennifer: drive the integration of CH into existing events. Jennifer also helps develop track outlines and participant journeys.
